# Quarterly commentary extracts

The input contract is finalized Markdown extracts from the Risk Commentary Warehouse.
Inspect headings, source-record entries, event dates, desk/perimeter/metric labels,
evidence IDs, review notes and managerial-validation statements. Trusted analysis
counts and screens supplied text and retains exact line locators. It does not fetch
underlying daily risk or PnL data. A commentary trigger is a selected movement-review
population, not a full daily risk population or proof of an absolute limit breach.
Missing evidence IDs, ambiguous quarters, repeated text and absent validation remain
limitations; do not manufacture joins or numerical corroboration. See policy.md for
interpretation and independent challenge requirements.
