# Cross-Asset Market Making Desk

## Desk scope
Products: equity derivatives, rates, credit, FX, and portfolio hedges.
Currencies: EUR, USD, JPY, GBP, and mapped emerging-market exposures.
Review period: 2025-07-01 through 2026-06-30.

## Control framework
Daily VaR, SVaR, stress, and exposure limits are monitored in SGMR.
Colibris records excess workflow, explanation, validation, and limit-change events.
All permanent limit changes require desk-head and risk pre-approval before effect.
Post-trade overrides require recorded approval before closure.

## Reporting conventions
Commentary extracts contain selected movement-review records, not all daily observations.
Movement thresholds are distinct from absolute risk limits.
Hierarchy and unit comparisons must remain at a documented comparable grain.
